# Leave Policy 2026

Version: 3.1
Created: 2026-01-15
Updated: 2026-08-01
Department: HR
Classification: Internal

## Overview

This policy outlines the leave entitlements for all full-time employees.

## Annual Leave

- **Engineers (Junior)**: 20 days per year
- **Engineers (Senior)**: 25 days per year
- **Managers**: 25 days per year
- **Directors**: 30 days per year
- **VP and above**: 30 days per year

## Sick Leave

- **All employees**: 10 days per year
- Unused sick leave does not carry over
- Medical certificate required for absences > 3 consecutive days

## Personal Leave

- **All employees**: 5 days per year
- Must be requested 2 weeks in advance
- Subject to manager approval

## Parental Leave

- **Primary caregiver**: 16 weeks paid
- **Secondary caregiver**: 4 weeks paid
- Must be taken within 12 months of birth/adoption

## Bereavement Leave

- **Immediate family**: 5 days paid
- **Extended family**: 2 days paid

## How to Request Leave

1. Submit request via HR portal
2. Manager approves/rejects
3. HR processes the request
4. Calendar is updated

## Contact

For questions about leave policy, contact hr@company.com.

# Day 12 — Business Alignment & Your AI Architect Portfolio

## Objective

Learn the senior half of AI Architecture: turning technical architecture into measurable business value.

## Business alignment

Learn to communicate:

- cost
- risk
- expected value
- time to market
- operational impact
- scalability
- strategic flexibility

## Define success before deployment

Example metrics:

- cost per successful task
- resolution rate
- response latency
- automation percentage
- user satisfaction
- human escalation rate
- error rate
- revenue impact
- operational savings

## Build vs Buy

Always ask:

- What problem are we solving?
- Is AI necessary?
- What is the cheapest acceptable solution?
- What must remain differentiated?
- What should be managed by a vendor?
- What capabilities should remain in-house?

## Final exercise

Create a one-page architecture decision record for the system you designed across the 12 days.

Include:

- architecture summary
- key decisions
- alternatives
- risks
- governance
- security
- scalability
- cost
- success metrics
- business outcome

## Final portfolio

Your portfolio should now contain:

- architecture diagrams
- ADRs
- cost model
- capacity plan
- reliability strategy
- observability plan
- security threat model
- governance checklist
- business-value scorecard

## Final test

You are moving toward AI Architecture when you can explain not only:

"What did we build?"

but:

"Why did we build it this way, what alternatives did we reject, what will it cost, what can fail, how will we secure it, how will it scale, and how will we know it created value?"

## Final principle

Tools change.

Models change.

Cloud services change.

Architecture judgment compounds.
